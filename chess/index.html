Opening: French Defense: Rubinstein Variation — unfamiliar. Chris is more accustomed to Open or Closed Tarrasch positions.

Mistakes:

  • Did not choose the fianchetto/opposite-side-castling setup that would have better challenged Black's likely setup.
  • Declined a free a-pawn because of concern about rook activity on the a-file, even though Black had no adequate compensation and White had not castled.
  • Pushed pawns around a pinned bishop, losing a pawn and allowing an outside passed pawn.

Biggest turning point: The pawn pushes around the pinned bishop that lost a pawn and created the outside passer.

Main lesson: In unfamiliar positions, judge concrete compensation rather than avoiding material gains because of vague activity; be especially careful with pawn moves around pinned pieces.
